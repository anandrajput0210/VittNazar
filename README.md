\# VittNazar



\## Promise-to-Term Verification for Financial Sales



VittNazar helps retail investors compare financial sales promises

with official product documentation.



\### Core Workflow



Sales Pitch

\-> Claim Extraction

\-> Official Document

\-> Claim-to-Term Matching

\-> Supported / Contradicted / Not Found

\-> Promise Receipt

\-> Clarification Questions



\### Target User



First-time retail investors who may have difficulty understanding

financial sales communication and product documentation.



\### Primary SANGYAN Track



Track B - Investor Awareness, Rights \& Grievance Redressal



\### Supporting Track



Track E - Misinformation \& Financial Content Literacy



\### Core Principle



VittNazar does not provide investment recommendations.

It helps investors understand and verify claims before making

a financial decision.
